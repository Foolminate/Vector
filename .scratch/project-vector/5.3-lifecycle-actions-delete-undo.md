# Issue: Lifecycle Actions: Delete & Undo

## What to build
Implement manual deletion and a staged-change undo mechanism in the TUI, along with focus management refinements.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Add interactive lifecycle actions to the TUI, allowing users to mark jobs for deletion, undo staged changes, and navigate more smoothly.

**Current behavior:**
Users can promote/reject jobs, but cannot delete them or undo a staged change without restarting the app or manual DB intervention. Focus is sometimes lost when switching views.

**Desired behavior:**
- Keybinding `x` marks a job for deletion with a `🗑️` icon.
- Keybinding `Backspace` clears any staged change for the selected row.
- `action_save` performs hard deletion for jobs marked as `deleted`.
- Focus automatically returns to the job list when navigating back from Digests or Suggestions.

**Key interfaces:**
- **`src/review_tui.py`:** Update `ReviewApp` keybindings and staged change logic.
- **`src/database.py`:** Ensure `delete_job` or similar exists for hard deletion.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Keybinding `x` marks a job for deletion with a `🗑️` icon.
- [ ] Keybinding `Backspace` clears any staged change for the selected row.
- [ ] `action_save` performs hard deletion for jobs marked as `deleted`.
- [ ] Focus automatically returns to the job list when navigating back from Digests or Suggestions.
