# Project Vector: Technical Doctrine
**Version:** 1.0

## Must-Haves (Scale Indicators)
Roles must demonstrate strategic oversight, automation, or architectural impact. Positive indicators include:
- **Automation:** Presence of "Python", "SQL", "APIs", or "ETL" in the context of building/improving systems.
- **Architecture:** Mentions of "System Design", "Data Modeling", or "Technical Roadmap".
- **Strategy:** "Process Optimization", "Digital Transformation", or "Scaling Operations".
- **Product Management:** "Technical Product Management", "Lifecycle Management", or "Stakeholder Influence".

## Hard-Disqualifiers (Toil Indicators)
Roles focused on manual operational overhead are disqualified. Negative indicators include:
- **Manual Toil:** "Data Entry", "Manual Reconciliation", "Invoice Processing", "Day-to-day administrative tasks".
- **Tooling:** Heavy reliance on "Excel" or "Spreadsheets" as the *primary* driver of the role without automation.
- **Accounting/Finance Focus:** Roles requiring "CPA", "CA", or purely focused on financial reconciliation.

## Weighting-Factors
- **Geographic Bonus:** Roles located in **Hamilton** or the **Waikato** region receive a +2 score boost.
- **Commute Penalty:** Roles in **Auckland** receive a -5 score penalty unless explicitly tagged as "100% Remote".
- **Remote Reward:** Roles verified as **100% Remote** are treated as High Priority regardless of company location.
- **Management vs. Execution:** "Manager" or "Lead" titles are welcome as long as they focus on Technical Strategy rather than pure People Management or Admin.
